# Fujifilm X-T1 XPan Mask

An experimental firmware patch that turns the Fujifilm X-T1's "HD Framing" guideline into a 65:24 XPan mask in the viewfinder and on the LCD screen.

Instructions and the patch kit are at https://xt1-xpan.gregdev.com (the kit's files are also in [xt1-xpan-mask-kit](xt1-xpan-mask-kit)).

Use at your own risk. This modifies camera firmware and has only been tested on one camera. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Fujifilm or Hasselblad.
